## 1. Creative Producer (Film/TV/Stage)

**EOI Category:** `producer`
**Optimal Journey:** Home > Facilities > Creative > EOI (producer)

### Entry Expectations
- Arrives looking for a production facility capable of handling large-scale film, TV, or stage productions.
- Expects professional-grade specifications: stage dimensions, soundproofing ratings, LED volume capabilities.
- Likely referred by industry contacts or discovered through trade publications.

### Page-by-Page Content Needs

| Page | What They Look For |
|------|-------------------|
| **Home** | Overview of the facility's scale and ambition. Key differentiator: integrated ecosystem vs. traditional studio lots. |
| **Facilities** | Detailed specs: stage dimensions (sqm/sqft), ceiling height, soundproofing (STC/NRC), LED configurations, ancillary spaces. Comparison to known facilities (Fox Studios, Docklands). |
| **Creative** | Service integration model. How pre-production, production, and post-production connect. Whether in-house services reduce vendor coordination. |
| **Contact** | Direct path to register production interest with category-specific fields (company, production type, timeline). |

### Decision Points
- Stage specifications meet their production requirements.
- Cost efficiency of integrated ecosystem vs. multi-vendor approach.
- Timeline alignment with their production schedule.
- Geographic advantages (Queensland incentives, timezone coverage).

### Conversion Goal
EOI submission with `category: "producer"`, including company name, production type, and timeline in metadata.

### Content Tone
Professional, specification-driven, industry-standard terminology. Avoid marketing superlatives. Lead with measurable capabilities.

## 2. Investor

**EOI Category:** `investor`
**Optimal Journey:** Home > Vision > Community (sustainability) > EOI (investor)

### Entry Expectations
- Evaluating Production City as an investment opportunity.
- Looking for market analysis, team credibility, revenue model indicators, and global strategy.
- Expects forward-looking statements with appropriate disclaimers.

### Page-by-Page Content Needs

| Page | What They Look For |
|------|-------------------|
| **Home** | Scale of ambition, brand credibility, key claims substantiated. |
| **Vision** | Global network strategy, campus expansion roadmap, team credentials (Troy Kelly bio), stakeholder benefits for investors specifically. |
| **Community** | Sustainability commitments (ESG alignment), education partnerships (talent pipeline = operational de-risk), government relationships. |
| **Contact** | Investment-focused EOI with organisation and role fields. |

### Decision Points
- Credibility of the founding team.
- Geographic diversification strategy (Queensland > Singapore > Hawaii > Europe > USA).
- Revenue model viability (integrated ecosystem reduces client churn).
- ESG/sustainability alignment for fund mandates.

### Conversion Goal
EOI submission with `category: "investor"`, including organisation and role in metadata.

### Content Tone
Financial, strategic, measured. Appropriate use of forward-looking statement disclaimers. No hype. Data-driven where possible.

## 3. Government / Policy

**EOI Category:** `partner` (not `investor`)
**Optimal Journey:** Home > Community > Vision > EOI (partner)

### Entry Expectations
- Assessing economic impact: job creation, foreign investment attraction, skills development.
- Looking for alignment with regional development objectives and education partnerships.
- May be evaluating incentive/grant eligibility.

### Page-by-Page Content Needs

| Page | What They Look For |
|------|-------------------|
| **Home** | Scale, location (Queensland focus), economic significance claims. |
| **Community** | Education partnerships, sustainability commitments, employment projections, community benefit. Bridges section: "For Government Bodies" (jobs, tourism, upskilling, research partnerships, foreign investment). |
| **Vision** | Global network with Queensland as anchor. Stakeholder benefits for "Governments & Communities". |
| **Contact** | Partnership EOI. Government/Policy should map to `partner` category with organisation field. |

### Decision Points
- Job creation numbers (direct and indirect).
- Education institution partnerships (existing or planned).
- Sustainability and environmental commitments.
- Alignment with state/federal development priorities.

### Conversion Goal
EOI submission with `category: "partner"`, including organisation and partnership area in metadata.

### Content Tone
Economic impact language. Quantitative where possible. Formal, policy-aligned. Emphasise public benefit.

## 8. Potential Employee

**EOI Category:** `employment` (NEW)
**Optimal Journey:** Home > Creative > Community > Contact

### Entry Expectations
- Looking for roles, culture, and what it's like to work at Production City.
- Wants to understand what positions will be available and how to apply.
- May be exploring before formal recruitment begins.

### Page-by-Page Content Needs

| Page | What They Look For |
|------|-------------------|
| **Home** | What the organisation does. Scale and ambition (signals job stability). |
| **Creative** | What disciplines and departments exist. The 17 service areas indicate the breadth of roles. |
| **Community** | Culture signals: education commitment, sustainability, community engagement. Employee development opportunities. |
| **Contact** | Employment EOI with desired role, experience level, availability, and optional portfolio URL. |

### Decision Points
- Roles match their skills and experience.
- Culture and values alignment.
- Growth and development opportunities.
- Location and availability alignment.

### Conversion Goal
EOI submission with `category: "employment"`, including desiredRole (required), experienceLevel, availability, and portfolioUrl in metadata.

### Content Tone
Professional but warm. Emphasise culture, growth, and the opportunity to build something from the ground up.
